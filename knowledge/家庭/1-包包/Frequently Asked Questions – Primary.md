# Frequently Asked Questions – Primary

**Frequently Asked Questions – Primary**



**What does the admissions assessment involve?**

The admissions assessment will last around:

* 1 hour for Primary 1 & 2,
* 1.5 hours for Primary 3 & 4, and
* 2 hours for Primary 5 & 6 applicants. 

Your child will be required to complete three written assessments - one for English Language, one for Chinese Language and one for Mathematics.



**What should I do if my child only reads and writes in traditional Chinese?**

Here at SISHK, Putonghua is taught using the simplified script for teaching Chinese. For children who are not familiar with simplified script, there will be test papers written in traditional script and answers can be written in traditional Chinese. Please note that your child will be expected to learn and use the simplified Chinese if he/she successfully enrols into SISHK.

The School would have sought parent's confirmation on the Chinese script prior to the assessment day. Please contact the Admissions Team if you have not been contacted on this matter.



**How do I prepare my child for the assessment?**

A few days before the assessment is due to take place, have a chat with your child to let them know that you will be visiting a new school together. Inform them that they will be asked to complete some worksheets in English, Chinese and Mathematics. 

The night before the assessment, you may wish to have the same conversation with your child to allay your child's anxiety, and address any concerns that she or he may have.



**What should I bring on the assessment day?**

* Your child's identification document (e.g. HKID/passport) for verification purposes
* A copy of this email as proof of your assessment invitation (in hard/soft copy)
* Stationery (pencils, erasers, ruler)
* Face masks, hand sanitizers, and water bottle for personal use (optional)



**What do I do if my child couldn't make it on the assessment day?**

We would be very grateful if you could inform the Admissions Team as soon as possible about your child's absence. If he/she is unwell, please supplement the School with a valid medical certificate. If the absence is due to unforseen circumstances, please supplement the School with the necessary supporting documents for verification.

Once the School has reviewed the reason for your child's absence and validated its authenticity, a new assessment date and time will be provided to your child at a later date. You can contact the Admissions Team at [admissions@singapore.edu.hk](mailto:admissions@singapore.edu.hk)



**How will the School communicate information to me?** 

Please note that the School will only be sending out notifications to the email addresses that were provided in the application form. The Admissions Team will mainly contact you via email. To ensure that you receive the School's emails and to prevent our emails ending up in your spam folders, please add our email address [admissions@singapore.edu.hk](mailto:admissions@singapore.edu.hk) to your contact list.



**How do I get to the School?**

The School is conveniently located near the Wong Chuk Hang MTR station. Please proceed to the _**Secondary Section**_ _at_ _**2 Police School Road, Wong Chuk Hang**_ for registation.

Please note that there is no parking available at the School. For more information on public transportation and directions to the School, please click [here](https://singapore.us16.list-manage.com/track/click?u=007f0b2c8866189e7e2085769&id=464aa6c831&e=77c0442c6b).
